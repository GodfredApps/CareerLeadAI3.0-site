## Executive Summary

**CareerLead AI** is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed specifically for the African job market, starting with Ghana, Nigeria, and Kenya. We provide personalized career recommendations, CV optimization, skill assessments, and job matching services tailored to African professionals, students, and career changers.

### The Opportunity

- **Market Size:** 450M+ workforce in Sub-Saharan Africa
- **Target Segment:** 120M+ university students and young professionals (ages 18-35)
- **Problem:** Lack of accessible, culturally relevant career guidance in Africa
- **Solution:** AI-powered platform with local job market intelligence and contextual guidance

## Key Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: User Acquisition Cost
**Risk:** CAC higher than projected
**Mitigation:**
- Organic growth through content marketing (blog + SEO)
- Ambassador programs (low-cost acquisition)
- Referral incentives

### Risk 2: AI API Costs
**Risk:** Gemini API costs scale faster than revenue
**Mitigation:**
- Implement usage limits for free tier
- Cache common responses
- Optimize prompts for token efficiency
- Consider hybrid approach (simpler logic for basic queries)

### Risk 3: Payment Infrastructure
**Risk:** Limited payment options in Africa
**Mitigation:**
- Integrate Paystack, Flutterwave (local payment gateways)
- Mobile money integration (M-Pesa, MTN, Airtel)
- Annual subscriptions (lower transaction frequency)

### Risk 4: Competition
**Risk:** Global players enter market
**Mitigation:**
- Build strong local partnerships
- Focus on cultural relevance and local insights
- Move fast to establish brand presence
